Invoice Import

This invoice will be added to the selected supplier tab and current location: Waverley. Upload a PDF invoice, CSV/TXT file, or paste invoice lines. For PDF invoices the app will read the supplier item description and quantity column, including Bidfood Quantity Supplied, Wingers Qty Delivered, and Lesta Quantity. Manual format: Item, Quantity. Example: Tenders, 4.
